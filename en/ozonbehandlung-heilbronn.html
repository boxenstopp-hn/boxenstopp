When is ozone treatment useful?

Air conditioning smells

Musty smells when switching on ventilation may come from the A/C, evaporator or cabin filter.

Vehicle used by smokers

Smoke odour settles into upholstery, headliners, carpets and air vents. Ozone can help reduce it.

Pet smell

Dog and other animal odours can be persistent, particularly in carpets and upholstery.

Dampness

Musty smells often result from moisture, wet carpets, leaks or long periods without use.

Used cars

A cabin refresh can be worthwhile before selling or after buying a used car.

After cleaning

After interior cleaning, ozone treatment may help reduce remaining odours.

Ozone treatment for air conditioning smells

Bad A/C smells often start in the ventilation system, evaporator or cabin filter. Ozone can reduce cabin odours, especially when combined with A/C servicing or a new cabin filter.

If the A/C also fails to cool well, the technical system should be checked and serviced rather than only treating the smell.

Frequently asked questions about ozone treatment

How much does ozone treatment cost?

Ozone treatment starts at €39. The price depends on vehicle size, odour type and interior condition.

Does ozone help with smoke smells?

It can substantially reduce smoke odour. A heavily affected interior may also need cleaning.

Does ozone help with dog or pet smells?

Yes, it may reduce animal odours. Hair, dirt and dampness should be removed first.

Does ozone help with A/C smells?

It may help reduce musty smells from ventilation. A/C servicing or a new cabin filter is often helpful as well.

Does ozone remove every smell completely?

Not always. Heavy soiling, dampness and mould need to be treated at their source.

Does the car need cleaning beforehand?

The interior should be clean and dry. Remove rubbish and wet objects and dry seats, carpets and upholstery completely.

Can the smell return?

Yes, if the source remains, such as leaks, moisture, an old filter or dirty upholstery.

Can ozone be combined with A/C service?

Yes, particularly if the ventilation smells musty or servicing is overdue.
