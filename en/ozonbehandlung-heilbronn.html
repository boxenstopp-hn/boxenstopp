Frequently asked questions

Does ozone remove every smell permanently?

No. Ozone can reduce odours but persistent causes such as water leaks, mould or dirty upholstery need to be addressed.

Does the interior need cleaning first?

Yes, particularly if there are wet carpets or heavy contamination.

Can I combine ozone with A/C service?

Yes, this may be useful if the odour is associated with the ventilation system.
