Common DPF fault codes

Many DPF enquiries involve specific codes linked to the filter, pressure sensors, regeneration, exhaust backpressure or implausible sensor values.

P2002

DPF efficiency too low. This may involve filter loading, differential pressure, regeneration or exhaust faults. Read more: fault code P2002.

P242F

Heavy ash or soot loading. Cleaning may be worthwhile depending on the filter's condition. See fault code P242F.

P2452 / P2453

Differential pressure sensor fault or implausible readings. Check the sensor, hoses and filter. The differential pressure sensor may be faulty.

P2458

Regeneration takes too long. The filter may be heavily loaded or regeneration may be failing.

P2463

High soot loading in the particulate filter. This may indicate a blockage or failed DPF regeneration may be occurring.

P244A / P244B

Differential pressure too low or high, possibly caused by sensors, pipes, filter condition or exhaust backpressure.

A fault code alone is not always enough. Registration details, mileage, diagnostic reports and a short symptom description help.

When should you clean a DPF?

DPF warning light

If the DPF light comes on, soot loading may be excessive. Take particular care if the DPF light is flashing.

Limp mode and power loss

A blocked filter can increase backpressure, reduce performance and trigger limp mode.

Regeneration stops

If regeneration repeatedly starts but does not complete, the filter may be too heavily loaded.

High fuel consumption

Frequent regeneration or exhaust faults can increase consumption and reduce driveability.

Black exhaust smoke

Smoke or smells can indicate exhaust or combustion problems.

Replacing the DPF would be expensive

Before replacement, it is often worth checking DPF cleaning costs.

Common causes of a blocked DPF

Short trips

On short journeys, the vehicle may not reach the temperature needed for full regeneration.

Faulty sensors

Differential pressure sensor, faulty exhaust temperature sensor or other components can send incorrect readings.

EGR problems

A dirty EGR system can increase soot; it may help to clean the EGR valve.

Oil consumption

High oil consumption increases ash loading and can shorten DPF service life.

Injection problems

Faulty injectors or poor combustion can create more soot and block the DPF faster.

Interrupted regeneration

If the EGR valve is faulty or other faults are present, regeneration may stop early.

Frequently asked questions about DPF cleaning

How much does DPF cleaning cost?

Cleaning of a removed filter starts at €179. Total cost depends on whether removal and refitting are needed, the vehicle and the work required. Please send registration details and mileage.

When is cleaning better than replacement?

If a filter is blocked but not melted or mechanically broken, cleaning may be more economical.

What does fault code P2002 mean?

P2002 often indicates low DPF efficiency. Causes may include soot loading, regeneration, differential pressure or exhaust faults.

What does P242F mean?

P242F often indicates high ash or soot loading. Cleaning may be appropriate depending on the filter's condition.

Can I keep driving with a DPF warning?

Do not ignore a DPF warning. If limp mode, power loss or flashing warnings occur, arrange a prompt inspection.

Does the DPF have to be removed?

For thorough cleaning, the DPF is normally removed and cleaned outside the vehicle.

How long does cleaning take?

It depends on the vehicle, removal work and filter condition. We can estimate when you enquire.

How long will a cleaned DPF last?

It depends on the cause. If sensors, EGR, injection, oil use or driving conditions remain problematic, it may block again.

Can every DPF be cleaned?

No. If the filter is broken, melted or internally damaged, replacement may be necessary.
