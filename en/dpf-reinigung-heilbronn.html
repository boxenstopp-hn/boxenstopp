Diesel particulate filter cleaning near Heilbronn

The DPF traps soot from exhaust gases. Normally this soot burns off during regeneration. Short journeys, faulty sensors, injection or EGR faults, oil consumption and interrupted regeneration can cause a blockage.

This can lead to loss of power, limp mode, a DPF warning light, an engine warning light, higher fuel consumption or fault codes. Professional DPF cleaning can improve flow if the DPF is blocked .

Important: Cleaning does not replace a proper diagnostic assessment. If the cause is not repaired, the DPF warning light may reappear soon.
