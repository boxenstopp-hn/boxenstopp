Frequently asked questions

Can I bring only the removed filter?

Yes. The starting price of €179 applies to a filter delivered already removed.

Do you also remove and refit the DPF?

This may be possible depending on the vehicle, capacity and access. We prepare an individual quotation.

Will cleaning solve the original fault?

Not necessarily. A DPF can block again if the regeneration problem, EGR fault, sensor fault or another underlying cause remains.
