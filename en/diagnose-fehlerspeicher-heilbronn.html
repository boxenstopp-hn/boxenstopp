Reading fault codes near Heilbronn

When a warning lamp lights up, scanning the fault memory is often the first step. Stored codes may point to sensors, engine management, the exhaust system, airbags, ABS, ESP, AdBlue or the DPF and other systems.

A fault code alone is not a completed repair diagnosis. It indicates a direction, but the underlying cause often requires further checks. Proper diagnostics is better than replacing parts blindly.

Important: Clearing fault codes does not fix the problem. If the warning returns, the underlying fault must be investigated.

Typical warning lights and symptoms

Check engine light

The engine light can indicate exhaust faults, oxygen sensors, ignition, mass airflow, EGR, DPF, catalytic converters or many other issues.

ABS / ESP warning

Possible causes include wheel speed sensors, steering angle sensors, brake light switches, wiring and electrical connections.

Airbag warning light

Airbag faults may involve wiring connectors, seat occupancy sensors, pretensioners, clock springs or control units.

AdBlue fault

AdBlue faults can involve NOx sensors, pumps, tanks, heaters, dosing or system control.

Glow plug warning flashing

On diesels, a flashing glow plug light may indicate engine management, exhaust system, DPF, EGR or sensor faults.

Limp mode

In limp mode, the car reduces power to avoid damage. Causes can include the turbo, DPF, sensors or exhaust faults.

Common diagnostic fault codes

Many drivers search for a specific code related to engine lights, DPF problems, EGR, turbo, oxygen sensors, catalytic converters or misfires.

P0420

Catalyst efficiency below threshold. Potential causes include the catalyst, oxygen sensor, exhaust leaks or mixture faults.

P2002

Diesel particulate filter efficiency low, often related to the DPF, pressure, regeneration or exhaust system.

P242F

DPF ash loading or heavy soot accumulation indicating a blocked filter.

P0401

EGR flow insufficient. Causes can include EGR valve deposits, vacuum leaks, sensors or intake/exhaust problems.

P0299

Boost pressure too low. Possible causes include the turbocharger, vacuum system, boost hose or sensors.

P0300

Misfire detected. Possible causes include spark plugs, coils, fuel injection, compression or mixture formation.

P0101

Mass airflow signal implausible. Causes may include the airflow meter, air leaks, intake, wiring or software.

P0171 / P0172

Fuel mixture too lean or rich, possibly from air leaks, oxygen sensors, fuel pressure, injection or airflow sensor.

P2452 / P2453

DPF differential pressure sensor signal problem, common in DPF diagnostics, exhaust backpressure or sensor/pipe issues.

Note: The exact meaning of a code depends on the vehicle manufacturer, engine and control unit. Registration details or HSN/TSN help.

Frequently asked questions about diagnostics

How much does fault code reading cost?

The cost depends on the vehicle and work required. Simple reading can be quick, while fault tracing may take longer.

Can fault codes simply be deleted?

Technically often yes, but an unresolved fault will return. Clearing codes is not a repair.

Why is the check engine light on?

The engine light can be triggered by an oxygen sensor, catalyst, EGR, DPF, airflow meter, ignition, injection or air leak.

Can I drive with the engine light on?

It depends on the fault. If the engine jerks, loses power, the lamp flashes or limp mode is active, stop driving unnecessarily and arrange inspection.

What is limp mode?

The car reduces power to protect the engine, transmission or exhaust system. Have the cause checked promptly.

Why is the glow plug light flashing?

On diesels it can indicate issues with engine management, exhaust, DPF, EGR, sensors or limp mode.

What does an AdBlue fault mean?

Possible causes include NOx sensors, AdBlue pump, tank, heater, dosing system, hoses or control units.

Why is the ABS/ESP light on?

Common causes include wheel speed sensors, steering angle sensor, wiring, connectors or control units.

What does P0420 mean?

P0420 usually indicates poor catalyst efficiency. The cause may be the catalyst, oxygen sensor, leaks or incorrect mixture.

What does P2002 mean?

P2002 is often linked to the diesel particulate filter . Causes include loading, regeneration, pressure or exhaust faults.

What does P0401 mean?

P0401 often indicates insufficient EGR flow. Causes may include EGR valve, deposits, vacuum or sensors.

Can all faults be found immediately?

Not always. Intermittent faults may depend on temperature, load, humidity or driving conditions, requiring more tests.

What details do you need for an enquiry?

Registration details, mileage, warning lamp photo, any fault codes and a short description of when the problem occurs.
