Frequently asked questions

Does reading fault codes always identify the cause?

No. A stored fault code points to a system but additional measurements or checks may be necessary.

Do you perform all repairs?

Not every repair is offered. We discuss available diagnostic work and recommend a specialist where necessary.

What should I bring?

Vehicle details, a description of the symptoms and any previous diagnostic reports.
