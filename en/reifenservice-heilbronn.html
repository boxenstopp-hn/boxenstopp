When is tyre service useful?

Seasonal wheel change

Change complete summer and winter wheels at the start of the season; checks are available on request.

New tyres

If the tread is worn, tyres are damaged or old, new tyres should be fitted and balanced.

Vibrations when driving

If the steering wheel shakes, wheel imbalance may be one cause.

TPMS warning light

If the tyre pressure warning appears, the cause may involve pressure, sensors or TPMS programming.

Uneven tyre wear

If tyres wear on their inside or outside edges, the wheel geometry should be checked, possibly requiring wheel alignment.

Loss of tyre pressure

A tyre that repeatedly loses pressure may have a damaged valve, wheel rim, tyre sidewall or a puncture.

TPMS sensors and tyre pressure monitoring

Many newer vehicles have direct tyre pressure monitoring with sensors inside the wheels. During tyre or wheel changes, sensors may need testing, installation or programming.

A TPMS warning does not always indicate a damaged tyre. It may relate to pressure, sensor battery, programming or vehicle recognition.

Summer, winter or all-season tyres?

Summer tyres

Summer tyres are designed for warm conditions and can provide good grip and handling when conditions are suitable.

Winter tyres

Winter tyres are useful in cold, snowy or wintry weather and should be fitted before the season.

All-season tyres

All-season tyres can be convenient for normal use without extreme weather conditions.

Tip: With four identical tyre sizes, periodically rotating front and rear wheels can even out wear. This does not apply where different tyre sizes are fitted on each axle.

Frequently asked questions about tyre service

How much is a wheel change?

Changing four complete wheels starts at €35. The price depends on the vehicle and any additional work.

How much is tyre fitting?

Fitting four tyres starts at €70. Size, rim, TPMS, low-profile, SUV or van requirements can affect the price.

Which tyre sizes can you fit?

Tyres from 13 to 24 inches, depending on the rim, tyre type and vehicle.

Do you work with alloy wheels?

Yes, both alloy and steel wheels. Sensitive or large wheels may require extra work.

Can you program TPMS sensors?

Yes, compatible tyre pressure sensors can be checked, fitted and programmed.

Why does my steering wheel vibrate?

Causes include imbalance, damaged tyres, bent rims, incorrect pressure or suspension faults.

Why do my tyres wear on one side?

Uneven wear can result from incorrect alignment, camber, faulty suspension or wrong pressure. A wheel alignment may be advisable.

What do you need for a tyre quotation?

Vehicle model, tyre size, rim diameter, TPMS information and whether you want a wheel change or tyre fitting.
