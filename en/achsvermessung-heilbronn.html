3D wheel alignment near Heilbronn

During wheel alignment, we measure wheel positions and compare them with the manufacturer's specifications, including toe, camber and other geometry values.

Incorrect values can make the car feel unstable, pull sideways or wear tyres unevenly. Common signs include crooked steering, pulling left or right, or tyre wear on inner or outer edges. Correct adjustment may prevent unnecessary wear and improve straight-line tracking.

Important: Alignment is particularly useful after suspension work, hitting a kerb or pothole, an accident, lowering, fitting new suspension parts or discovering unusual tyre wear.

When should wheel alignment be checked?

Crooked steering wheel

If the steering wheel is off-centre when driving straight, the wheel alignment may be incorrect.

Uneven tyre wear

Tyres wearing on one edge can indicate incorrect geometry. To prevent costly tyre damage, alignment should be checked promptly; ourtyre servicecan also help.

Vehicle pulls to one side

If your car pulls left or right, does not track straight or needs constant steering correction, its wheel geometry should be checked.

Alignment after lowering, kerb impacts or suspension work

Replacing suspension components can change wheel geometry. Lowering may also affect toe and camber, so an alignment check is recommended afterwards.

Hitting a kerb, pothole or having an accident can also affect wheel geometry. You may only notice it later through tyre wear, crooked steering, poor tracking or pulling to one side.

Frequently asked questions about wheel alignment

How much does wheel alignment cost?

Front axle alignment starts at €79. If the rear axle is adjustable and needs correction, an additional charge from €20 applies.

When should wheel geometry be adjusted?

Check alignment if the steering wheel is crooked, the car pulls sideways, tyres wear unevenly or suspension parts have been replaced.

Is alignment needed after lowering?

Yes. Lowering often changes the geometry, so an alignment check is recommended.

Why do tyres wear on the inner or outer edges?

Uneven wear may result from incorrect toe or camber, worn suspension parts or tyre pressure.

Can every car be adjusted?

Not every geometry value is adjustable on every model. We check what can be corrected and whether components move freely.

How long does wheel alignment take?

The time depends on the vehicle, bolt condition and adjustment work. Seized parts take longer.

What if suspension parts are damaged?

Worn tie rods, control arms, bushes or other suspension components should be repaired before adjustment.

Why does my car pull left or right?

Possible causes include alignment, tyre pressure, uneven tyre wear or worn suspension parts. Alignment helps check the wheel positions.

Should I check alignment after hitting a kerb?

Yes. After a hard kerb impact or pothole, a check is sensible even if you notice nothing at first.

Is alignment needed after fitting new tyres?

Not always. Check it if old tyres wore unevenly, steering is crooked or the car pulls sideways.

Can incorrect toe increase tyre wear?

Yes. Incorrect toe can cause faster, uneven wear on the inner or outer edges or across the tread.
