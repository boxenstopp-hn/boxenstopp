Car air conditioning service in Heilbronn – A/C recharge and maintenance

Is your car's air conditioning no longer cooling properly, blowing warm air or smelling musty? Boxenstopp Nordheim offers professional A/C servicing in the Heilbronn area for systems using R134a or R1234yf.

Air conditioning systems may gradually lose refrigerant over time. This can reduce cooling performance and, in serious cases, damage the compressor. We service and recharge your system professionally.

Air conditioning maintenance for both refrigerants (R134a and R1234yf)

Whether you drive an older or newer model, we have modern automatic A/C service equipment for the established refrigerantR134aas well as the newer refrigerantR1234yf, which is used in most new vehicles from 2017 onwards.

During servicing, we recover the refrigerant, evacuate the system under vacuum, perform a leak check and refill it to the manufacturer's specifications with the correct refrigerant and compressor oil.

Important: Too little refrigerant can impair compressor lubrication. Replacing a damaged compressor can cost well over €1,000.

What professional A/C servicing includes

Recovering refrigerant

Remaining refrigerant is recovered, weighed and processed, allowing us to determine the missing quantity.

Vacuum leak check

We evacuate the system and check for significant leaks before refilling.

Precise recharge

We add the correct amount of refrigerant and suitable compressor oil according to manufacturer specifications.

A/C disinfection and cabin filter

Condensation on the evaporator creates a damp environment where bacteria, mould and spores can develop. This can cause musty smells and aggravate allergies.

We therefore recommend cleaning the ventilation system and replacing the cabin pollen filter where appropriate, to improve air quality and airflow.

How an A/C service works in our workshop

Function and pressure check

We check the air outlet temperature in the cabin and measure operating pressures.

Recovery and drying

The refrigerant is recovered and moisture is removed using a vacuum.

Leak check

The system is held under vacuum for a specified period to identify possible leaks.

Recharge and oil addition

We carefully add refrigerant, suitable compressor oil and UV dye if required for leak detection.

Final performance check

We recheck cooling performance to confirm the system is working correctly.

Frequently asked questions about car A/C servicing

How much does A/C servicing cost?

R134a A/C service starts at €79 and R1234yf service starts at €149. Both starting prices include up to 200 g of refrigerant. Additional refrigerant is charged according to the required amount.

Why does an air conditioning system lose refrigerant?

Unlike a domestic refrigerator, a car has flexible hoses and compressor seals through which small amounts of refrigerant may escape over time.

Can I use air conditioning in winter?

Yes. Air conditioning removes moisture from the air and helps stop windows misting up. Regular use also helps maintain the system.

What is the difference between A/C servicing and A/C disinfection?

A/C servicing covers the technical system, refrigerant and compressor. Disinfection helps remove odours and microorganisms from the vehicle's ventilation system.
